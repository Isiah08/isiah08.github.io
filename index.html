Selected work · Austin, TX

Websites for businesses that sell something real.

Five concepts across home services, aesthetics, hospitality and professional services. Each one is built around whatever that trade actually sells on — a price list, a claim deadline, a short menu — rather than the same layout with a new logo dropped in.

Home services · Pressure washing

Hill Country Exterior Co.

This trade sells one thing: the hard edge between filthy and clean. So the hero headline sits inside a cleaned stripe cut through grimy concrete, and the page publishes per-square-foot rates instead of hiding behind "call for quote".

  • Published pricing
  • Light / industrial
  • Archivo Black + Public Sans

Aesthetics · Med spa

Lumen Aesthetics

Every med spa site shouts — gold foil, before/after grids, pastel cards. This one whispers. Treatments are set as a printed book index with leader dots and ranged-right pricing, and the palette is sage and porcelain rather than the usual cream and terracotta.

  • Index as interface
  • Editorial / quiet
  • Fraunces + Karla

Home services · Storm restoration

Ironhide Roofing

Roofing isn't sold on beauty, it's sold on a clock — the window to file a storm claim. Almost every competitor buries that. Here it's the spine of the page: a four-stage claim timeline running from date of loss to the policy deadline.

  • Deadline as structure
  • Heavy / industrial
  • Saira Condensed + IBM Plex

Hospitality · Steakhouse

Hearth & Hollow

Restaurants always open with a slideshow of food. This one opens with the menu, typeset as a letterpress card — because a fourteen-table room with one seating a night is arguing from its menu, not its photography. The single plate is demoted below the fold.

Professional services · Injury law

Vance & Rowe

No giant settlement figure, no partner with folded arms. The signature is a first-48-hours timeline — the part a frightened client actually needs — and there are deliberately no case results anywhere, because those are bar-regulated and fabricating them on a spec build would be dishonest.

  • Timeline as signature
  • Parchment / restrained
  • Newsreader + Source Sans 3

About these five. All are concept work for fictional businesses, built to demonstrate range. Every page carries a banner saying so. Pricing, menus and timelines are placeholders and are labelled on each page.

Photography is AI-generated. Regulated categories (medical, legal, insurance) carry the disclosures a live build would need.
