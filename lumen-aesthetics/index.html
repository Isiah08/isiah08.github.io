Approach

Four rules that don't bend.

  • One injector, every time

    You see the same person at every appointment. Nobody inherits your face halfway through a plan.

  • Conservative first pass

    Under-treating and reviewing at two weeks costs less than dissolving. It is also the only way to stay natural.

  • No packages, no pressure

    Nothing is sold in blocks of six. You book what you need, when you need it.

  • An honest no

    Some requests are better answered by skincare, a dermatologist, or nothing at all. That answer is free.
