FAQ

Fair questions.

Free? What does it actually cost me?

The build is free for the five founding businesses. Your only genuine cost is the domain — about $12 a year, in your name — since hosting for a site like this is free. Once the five spots are gone, the same build is a flat quoted rate.

How fast is "fast"?

The first working version of your site typically lands in days, not weeks — that's the version you judge before paying. Going live after that is usually within the week, mostly depending on how quickly your photos and details come through.

Okay — what's the catch?

Stated up front: an honest review once it's live, and permission to post the before/after as a case study. That's it. Five real results are worth more to me right now than five invoices, and you're the one who ends up with a website out of that maths.

Do you handle hosting and the domain?

Yes — I set up hosting, connect your domain, and wire up whatever booking tool you already use. You own everything: the domain, the files, the accounts.

What happens after launch?

Edits are free for the first month. After that, small changes are cheap and quoted per request. And if you want help turning the site into actual bookings — ads, content, follow-up — that's the direction I'm building toward, and founding clients get first refusal.

Can you redesign my existing site?

That's most of what I do. Send me the link — the before/after is usually the easiest sales pitch I have.
