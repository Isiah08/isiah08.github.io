Method

Pressure is the wrong tool
for most of your house.

High pressure belongs on flatwork and almost nowhere else. Using it on a roof or on siding is how you get stripped paint and voided warranties.

01

Flatwork — high pressure

Surface cleaner at 3,500 psi for concrete. Even passes, no zebra striping, edges cut by hand.

02

Siding — soft wash

Under 500 psi with a detergent dwell. The chemistry does the work, not the water.

03

Roofs — no pressure

Pure soft wash. Anything stronger lifts granules off the shingle and shortens the roof's life.

04

Every job — plants first

Beds pre-soaked and rinsed after. Detergent runoff kills landscaping faster than anything else.
