The fire

One fire. Lit at two.

Post oak, burned down to coals before service and worked all night. Every plate that leaves the pass has been over it — including the bread and most of the vegetables.

  • 45

    Days minimum on the dry-aging of every cut of beef we serve.

  • 14

    Tables. One seating. The room turns once, so the table is yours all night.

  • 0

    Gas burners in the building. If the fire goes out, service is over.
