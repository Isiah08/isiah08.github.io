Scope

Roofs, and the things that ruin roofs.

A leak is rarely just shingles. Everything below gets checked on the same visit because water travels before it shows.

  • 01

    Full replacement

    Tear-off to deck, new underlayment, architectural or impact-rated shingle. Decking replaced where it is soft, priced per sheet.

  • 02

    Storm repair

    Targeted repair where the damage is genuinely local — and an honest word when a repair is throwing money at a roof that needs replacing.

  • 03

    Flashing & penetrations

    Chimneys, valleys, vents and skylights. This is where most leaks actually start, not in the field of the roof.

  • 04

    Gutters & drainage

    Undersized or blocked gutters push water back under the shingle edge and rot the fascia from behind.
